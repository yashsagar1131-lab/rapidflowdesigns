How a pilot runs

You don't need to connect your loan software. A spreadsheet in and a spreadsheet out is enough to start.

  1. 1 Week 0

    Agree the call

    A 30-minute call to pick the call flow, the language and the wording. You approve the script before any borrower hears it.

  2. 2 Week 0

    Share a list

    An Excel sheet with name, phone number, EMI amount and due date. Nothing more is needed.

  3. 3 Weeks 1–4

    Calls go out

    Calls run within calling hours. You get a results sheet every evening and can listen to any call.

  4. 4 Week 4

    Compare the results

    A short report: how many were reached, how many promised, how many paid on time, against your current process.

The sheet your team gets each evening

One row per borrower called. Your staff start the next morning with the accounts that need a person.

Rules every call follows

Written into the assistant from the start, and checked with you before the pilot begins.

Calling hours only

Calls go out between 8 am and 7 pm, in line with RBI guidance on contacting borrowers.

It says it is automated

Every call opens by naming your company and saying an automated assistant is calling.

Every call is recorded

Borrowers are told at the start, and you can listen to any call.

Polite, every time

No threats, no pressure, no talk of consequences. Difficult conversations go to your staff.

The least data needed

Name, phone number, amount and due date. Nothing else from your loan files.

Your data stays yours

Used only for your calls, and deleted at the end of the pilot if you ask.

Questions lenders ask first

Do we need to connect our loan management system?

No. For the pilot, you send an Excel or CSV file with the accounts to call, and we send results back the same way. Connecting your system can come later if the pilot works.

Which languages does it speak?

Hindi and English, including the mix of the two most borrowers use. If your borrowers mostly speak another Indian language, tell us which one and we'll confirm before the pilot starts.

Will borrowers mind talking to an AI?

Some will prefer a person, and they can ask for one at any point. The pilot measures this directly: you'll see how many borrowers answered, stayed on the call and promised a date, compared with your own team's calls.

What does the pilot cost?

A small flat fee that covers setup and the calls for four weeks. It depends on the number of accounts and is agreed in writing before anything starts. There's no commitment after the pilot.

Who decides what the assistant says?

You do. We draft the script with you, you approve it, and nothing changes during the pilot without your sign-off.

Who is RapidFlow Designs?

A small AI implementation studio. These pilots are how we're building our first published results, so we keep the number small and run each one closely.
