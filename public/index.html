Rules every call follows

  • Calling hours only. 8 am to 7 pm.
  • Says it is automated and names your company.
  • Recorded, and you can listen to any call.
  • Polite. Difficult conversations go to your staff.
  • Minimum data. Name, phone, amount, due date.

Questions lenders ask first

Do we need to connect our loan system?

No. You send an Excel file and we send results back the same way.

Which languages does it speak?

Hindi and English, including the mix of the two. For another language, ask us before the pilot.

What if a borrower wants a person?

They can ask at any point. Your team gets a call-back request the same day.

Who decides what it says?

You do. You approve the script, and nothing changes mid-pilot without your sign-off.
